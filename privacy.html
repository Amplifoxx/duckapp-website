Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly, information created through your use of the service, and certain technical information necessary to operate, secure, and improve the app.

The categories of information described below may vary depending on which features you choose to use.

Account Information

When you create or maintain an account, we may collect information such as your email address, username, account identifier, authentication information, profile information, and account settings.

If third-party sign-in options are offered, we may receive information from the authentication provider that you authorize the provider to share with us, such as an account identifier, name, or email address.

Vehicle and License Plate Information

The service may allow users to provide or associate vehicle-related information with an account, including country, state or jurisdiction, license plate identifiers, and other vehicle information.

License plate information may also be submitted by another user when using features such as gifting or attempting to identify a vehicle within the service. As a result, a license plate identifier may be stored or processed before the owner or associated user has created an account or claimed that identifier.

We use this information to provide vehicle-based app functionality, associate eligible vehicles or identifiers with accounts, facilitate gifting and related interactions, prevent misuse, and operate the service.

QR Codes and Identifiers

The app may generate or process QR codes, account identifiers, vehicle identifiers, or similar codes used to connect users and app features.

These identifiers may be associated with account, vehicle, collection, or transaction information as necessary to provide the requested feature.

Collections and App Activity

We may collect and maintain information about your activity within the app, including ducks you own, duplicate ducks, collections, achievements, rewards, crates, Duck Bucks balances, gifting and trading activity, friends or other social connections, and related account history.

This information is used to maintain your account, provide app features, synchronize your collection, prevent fraud and abuse, and operate the virtual economy.

Purchases and Transactions

We may maintain records relating to purchases, virtual currency, crates, virtual items, transaction status, transaction identifiers, and related account activity.

Purchases made through an app store or other authorized payment provider are processed by the applicable provider. We may receive transaction information necessary to verify and fulfill a purchase, but payment providers may process payment-card, billing, or other financial information under their own privacy policies.

Gifting and Trading

When you gift, receive, or trade an eligible virtual item, we may process information about the participants, virtual items involved, vehicle or account identifiers used to facilitate the interaction, timestamps, status, and related transaction history.

We may retain appropriate transaction records for account integrity, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, and operation of the service.

Public and Social Information

Certain information may be visible to other users when you choose to participate in public or social features.

Depending on the features available, this may include your username, profile information, collection, selected ducks, achievements, or other information that the app identifies as visible to other users.

You should not include sensitive personal information in usernames, profile fields, or other information intended to be publicly visible.

Camera Access

The app may request access to your device's camera for features such as scanning QR codes.

Camera permission is requested through your device's permission system. You may deny or revoke camera access through your device settings, although features requiring the camera may not function without that permission.

Access to the camera does not by itself mean that photographs or video are uploaded or stored by us. If a future feature collects or uploads camera content, this Privacy Policy and any required in-app disclosures will be updated as appropriate.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, we or our service providers may process device or notification identifiers needed to deliver notifications about account activity, gifts, achievements, transactions, service information, or other app features.

You can control notification permissions through your device settings.

Technical and Usage Information

When you use the service, we and our service providers may automatically process technical information necessary to operate and secure the app.

Depending on the technologies used, this may include device and operating system information, app version, network information, IP address, timestamps, authentication events, error and crash information, diagnostic information, and interactions with app features.

How We Use Information

We may use information described in this Privacy Policy to:

  • Create, authenticate, maintain, and secure accounts.
  • Provide collections, gifting, trading, social, vehicle, QR, purchase, and virtual-item features.
  • Process and fulfill transactions.
  • Maintain Duck Bucks balances, collections, achievements, and account history.
  • Identify, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, exploits, unauthorized activity, and security threats.
  • Diagnose technical problems and maintain the reliability of the service.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Communicate service, security, account, or policy information.
  • Understand and improve the functionality and performance of the app.
  • Enforce our Terms of Use and other policies.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and protect our users, rights, property, and service.

How We Disclose Information

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to operate the service, including to service providers that perform functions on our behalf, such as hosting, database services, authentication, infrastructure, payment processing, app distribution, security, diagnostics, communications, and customer support.

We may also disclose information when you direct us to do so, when necessary to provide a feature involving another user, in connection with a corporate transaction, or when reasonably necessary to comply with law, enforce our agreements, protect rights or safety, investigate fraud or abuse, or respond to lawful legal process.

Service Providers

We may use third-party companies and services to operate portions of the app. These providers may process information on our behalf as necessary to perform their services.

We require service providers handling personal information on our behalf to protect that information in accordance with applicable requirements and the purposes for which it was provided. Third-party services may also have their own privacy policies governing information they process independently from us.

Other Users

Some app features necessarily involve sharing limited information with other users. For example, collection, profile, gifting, trading, friendship, or vehicle-based features may make certain information available to the users involved in those interactions.

Information you make publicly available or intentionally share with another user may be seen, copied, or retained by that user. We cannot control how another user independently uses information they receive outside the service.

Business Transfers

If The Duck App is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, sale of assets, transfer of the service, or similar transaction, information may be transferred or disclosed as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law.

Legal and Safety Disclosures

We may preserve, access, use, or disclose information when we reasonably believe doing so is necessary to comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request; enforce our agreements; detect or prevent fraud, security incidents, or abuse; protect the rights, property, or safety of The Duck App, our users, or others; or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide and secure the service, maintain your account, fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and abuse, enforce agreements, and protect our legitimate interests.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, the purpose for which it is processed, the status of an account, and applicable legal requirements.

When information is no longer reasonably necessary for these purposes, we may delete, anonymize, or otherwise dispose of it as appropriate and as required by applicable law.

Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your account through the account deletion features made available by the app. Deleting an account may permanently remove your account, collection, progress, virtual items, profile information, and other data associated with the account.

We may retain limited information after an account deletion request where retention is reasonably necessary or legally permitted for purposes such as fraud prevention, security, financial or transaction recordkeeping, compliance with legal obligations, dispute resolution, or the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims.

Information that has been lawfully anonymized or aggregated so that it no longer identifies you may also be retained.

Unclaimed Vehicle or Plate Information

Certain vehicle or license plate identifiers may be submitted to the service by a user before the associated person creates or claims an account. Such information may be retained as reasonably necessary to provide pending gifting or vehicle-based functionality, prevent abuse, maintain service integrity, or comply with applicable requirements.

Where required by applicable law, we will provide appropriate mechanisms for eligible individuals to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with them.

Your Choices

Depending on the feature and your location, you may be able to control certain information through account settings, device permissions, privacy controls, or requests submitted to us.

You may revoke device permissions, such as camera or notification permissions, through your device settings. Revoking a permission may prevent features that depend on that permission from functioning.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide rights concerning your personal information, which may include rights to request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of certain information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

These rights are subject to applicable exceptions and verification requirements. We may request information reasonably necessary to verify your identity or authority before completing certain requests.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights provided by applicable law.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no electronic transmission, storage system, network, or security measure can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your account credentials and for notifying us if you believe your account has been compromised.

Children's Privacy

The service is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If we learn that personal information from a child has been collected in a manner requiring parental consent without the required consent, we will take appropriate steps to delete or otherwise address the information as required by applicable law.

International Use

The service and its providers may process or store information in countries other than the country in which you reside.

Where required, we will use appropriate mechanisms or safeguards for transfers of personal information across jurisdictions.

Third-Party Services and Links

The service may contain links to or integrations with third-party services. This Privacy Policy does not govern the independent privacy practices of third parties when they process information for their own purposes.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of third-party services you choose to use.
