Answers to some of the most common questions about The Duck App.

ACCOUNT ACCESS

If you've forgotten your password, use the Forgot Password option on the login screen. For other account access problems, contact support.

DUCK BUCKS

Duck Bucks are virtual currency used within The Duck App. If a completed purchase does not appear in your balance, contact support and we'll help investigate.

CRATES & DUCKS

Crates contain randomized digital ducks. Available rarities and odds are displayed in the app before a crate is purchased.

PURCHASE ISSUES

Purchases on iPhone are processed through Apple's in-app purchase system. If you've been charged but something doesn't look right in the app, contact us.

DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT

You can request deletion of your account directly from the account settings inside The Duck App.

REPORT A PROBLEM

Found a bug or something that isn't working as expected? Email support with a description of what happened and, if possible, a screenshot.
